Nice to Meet You!

My Story & Philosophy

Hey, I'm Drew McKay.

Hey there! I’m so glad you found your way here. I started Zero Limit Productions with a really simple goal: to make filming high-quality videos relaxed, fun, and completely stress-free.

Whether I’m out capturing a wedding day packed with laughter and happy tears, filming a local theater production, or helping your business stand out with a creative commercial, I’m all about saving those real, genuine moments. I absolutely love making videos that make you feel something special every single time you hit play.

Since I personally run everything here, I get to treat your project with the absolute care it deserves. When you work with me, you don’t have to worry about a giant, intimidating camera crew crowding the room or making things feel stiff. I always keep the energy light, comfortable, and positive so you can just focus on enjoying the day while I quietly capture all the best parts.

What I Value

How I Do Things

I keep my process direct, friendly, and focused entirely on bringing your story to life beautifully.

Real, Open Connection

When you reach out, you talk directly to me. I love listening to your ideas, collaborating on your vision, and making sure all of your preferences are perfectly understood with no messy middleman.

Comfortable, Natural Vibes

The best footage happens when people feel totally at ease. I focus on being helpful and blend right into the background. This lets me catch candid smiles at events or relaxed, genuine expressions on a corporate set.

I balance my lighthearted style with beautiful technical execution. From crystal-clear professional audio recording to stunning color choices and smooth pacing, I ensure your final film looks amazing and stands the test of time.

Easy & Clear Timelines

I move quickly and respect your schedule. Commercial projects are turned around efficiently to fit perfectly with your launch calendars, while special milestone and family legacy videos are crafted with deep care to ensure every memory shines bright.
